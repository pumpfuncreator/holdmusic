Meet Holden

Holden got put on hold in 1987 and nobody ever came back. He learned the saxophone, made friends with everyone else in the queue, and now hosts the longest hold line on the internet.

Everyone is welcome. The wait is the point.

  • Endlessly polite"Thank you for your patience." He means it every time.
  • UnbotheredHe's been waiting for decades and made peace with it.
  • MusicalBig moments get a sax solo instead of words.
  • Secretly caringHe keeps the queue company so nobody waits alone.

Why this exists

I launched a coin before. Nobody came, because there was nothing to come for.

This time the community comes first. Hold Music starts as a character, a joke and a group of people who find it funny. A token might never exist, and the project keeps going either way.

Progress is shown in the open, with real numbers, in the queue below.
